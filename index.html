What we do

Fresh color at your front door, all year

Custom container gardens

Every planter is designed around your space, your light and your style, then planted and set in place on your porch or patio.

Seasonal porch refreshes

We change your planters with the seasons, from summer annuals to fall mums, pansies and pumpkins, then evergreens and berries for winter.

Wreaths, sashes & bows

Wreaths made for your door and the season, finished with a hand-tied bow or a monogrammed sash.

Interior plantscapes

Orchids, snake plants and other easy-care greenery that bring a little of the garden inside.

Beds & borders

Seasonal color for walkways, flower beds and borders all around your home.

Before & after

From faded to finished

A tired, dried-out planter doesn't have to be thrown away. We give old containers a second life, filling them with fresh plants and turning them into living works of art for your porch.

How it works

Simple from start to finish

  1. Reach out by email

    Send us a note about what you have in mind. We'll get back to you and work out the details together.

  2. We design it

    We choose plants, colors and containers that suit your space and your home.

  3. We plant & install

    We gather everything, plant it and set it up for you.

  4. Refresh each season

    When the season changes, we'll swap in fresh plants so your porch always looks its best.

Meet the owners

Two plant lovers, 50+ years of experience

Porchscapes by Carol & Susan is a business built by two plant lovers who turned a knack for beautiful containers into something they could share. Together, they bring more than 50 years of experience in growing, designing and arranging.

They keep up with new plants and ideas at industry events like Green & Growin', and every arrangement they create is designed with its home in mind.
